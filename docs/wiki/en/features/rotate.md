# Rotate pages

## What it does

Correct the orientation of selected pages or an entire PDF.

## How to use it

Pages are on the left; settings, destination, and the button are in the right
column. Turn with **ROTATE LEFT** or **ROTATE RIGHT**: each click adds a
quarter turn and the resulting angle is shown. The target is **All pages** by
default; choose **Selected pages** or **Active page** to narrow it. Check the
preview. A destination is proposed in the PDF’s folder; **CHANGE…** lets you
choose another one. Click **ROTATE PAGES**.

## Result

A new PDF contains the selected rotations. The result appears below the button, with **OPEN PDF**, **OPEN FOLDER** and **CONTINUE WITH…**. The source file
remains unchanged.

## Limits

Only 90, 180, and 270 degrees produce a file: at 0°, the button stays disabled
and explains why, since the PDF would be unchanged. The preview shows orientation but
does not edit page content.
