# Appearance

Under **Settings → Theme**, six choices are available:

- Automatic — follow system;
- Dark — Clean;
- Light — Clean;
- Dark — Pixel;
- Light — Pixel;
- High contrast.

Automatic mode follows the system’s light or dark theme. If that information is
unavailable, PriveoPDF uses **Dark — Clean**.

Reduced motion is also available in Settings. Appearance choices remain local
and never change PDF content or exported rendering.
