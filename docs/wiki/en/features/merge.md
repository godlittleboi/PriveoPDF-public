# Merge PDFs

## What it does

Combine several PDFs into one document.

## How to use it

Add at least two PDFs, place them in the required order, choose the destination,
and start the merge.

## Result

A new PDF contains the documents in the displayed order. Source files remain
unchanged.

## Limits

The destination must differ from every source and must not already exist. Some
bookmarks or complex structures may be simplified.
