# Insert pages

## What it does

Add pages from a second PDF to a main PDF.

## How to use it

Choose the main document, then the document containing pages to insert: all
its pages are selected. To keep fewer, click a page, then Ctrl+click or
Shift+click to adjust the selection. Choose the position. A destination is
proposed in the main document’s folder; **CHANGE…** lets you choose another one.

## Result

A new PDF keeps every page from the main document and adds the selection at the
chosen position. The result appears below the button, with **OPEN PDF**, **OPEN FOLDER** and **CONTINUE WITH…**. Both sources remain unchanged.

## Limits

The tool works with two PDFs at a time and keeps the selected pages in their
original order from the second document.
