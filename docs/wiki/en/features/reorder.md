# Reorder pages

## What it does

Change page order and duplicate selected pages when needed.

## How to use it

Drag thumbnails into the required order and use the duplicate action if needed.
A destination is proposed in the PDF’s folder; **CHANGE…** lets you choose another one.

## Result

A new PDF follows the visible order and contains the requested copies.
The result appears below the button, with **OPEN PDF**, **OPEN FOLDER** and **CONTINUE WITH…**. The source remains unchanged.

## Limits

Every original page must remain at least once. A single-page document has
nothing to reorder: the button stays greyed out with that reason. Use the dedicated tool to remove
pages or insert pages from another document.
